Privacy policy

Updated October 1, 2026

Your teams and calculations stay in the app. Ripply Apps does not receive them unless you choose to share them with us.

Your teams and calculations

Toto’s Toolkit stores team names, Pokémon, chosen moves, training, abilities, items, regulation labels, and retained import text on your device so you can reopen and edit your teams. Your theme choice is also saved locally. Calculations and temporary comparison choices are processed on your device.

The app has no player account, Ripply Apps cloud sync, advertising, analytics service, or automatic crash reporting. It does not send your teams or calculations to Ripply Apps. The core tools, battle data, and Pokémon sprites are bundled for offline use.

Files and clipboard

Import reads the team text or backup file you choose. On phones, the system file picker can copy the selected file into the app’s temporary cache. Export creates a local file and opens the system share sheet, or requests a browser download. Copy text writes your selected export to the clipboard.

A full backup can contain complete sets, team names, and original import text. The app does not upload backups automatically. If you choose a cloud drive, messaging app, or another sharing destination, that service handles the copy under its own terms.

Keeping or removing local data

Saved teams remain in app storage until you remove the app’s local data. On Android, use the phone’s app settings to clear Toto’s Toolkit storage; deleting the app also removes its local storage. Export a backup first if you want to keep your teams. In the browser preview, clear this site’s stored data through the browser’s settings.

Your device’s security and backup settings apply. Android may include app data in device backups. Clearing or uninstalling the app does not remove exported files, clipboard copies, copies you shared, or existing device/cloud backups; manage those in their own locations.

Optional support through Stripe

Support via Stripe opens the selected Ripply Apps checkout in your browser only when you tap the button. Opening the Support page alone does not contact Stripe. No payment details are entered into Toto’s Toolkit, and the app does not receive full card numbers or bank login details.

Stripe processes information entered into checkout and related transaction, device, and network information under its privacy policy. Ripply Apps can receive merchant transaction records, such as contribution amount, payment status, and contact details supplied at checkout, to handle support payments, receipts, refunds, and accounting. Payment records may be retained for applicable accounting, tax, or dispute obligations. Contributions do not unlock app features.

Support messages and external websites

If you contact Ripply Apps, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use it to respond and troubleshoot. Do not include passwords or payment credentials. We retain correspondence while needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations; you can contact us about access or deletion.

Credits, rule references, and Stripe privacy links open external websites when you tap them. Those websites and your browser handle their own network requests, cookies, and privacy practices.

Test builds and updates

An Expo Go preview runs inside Expo’s testing app and connects to the development server. Expo, the tunnel provider, and the testing host have their own data practices; this is separate from the offline standalone Android app.

We will update this policy when the app’s data handling changes. The date above identifies this version.
